Title: Cold Food Storage Chart
Source: FoodSafety.gov (USDA, FDA and CDC)
URL: https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/cold-food-storage-charts
Checked: November 2026

Keep the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below and the freezer at 0°F (-18°C) or below. Food kept frozen at 0°F stays safe indefinitely; the freezer times below are for best quality.

Raw ground beef, hamburger and other ground meats keep 1 to 2 days in the refrigerator and 3 to 4 months in the freezer.

Fresh beef and pork steaks, chops and roasts, such as ribeye steak or pork chops, keep 3 to 5 days in the refrigerator and 4 to 12 months in the freezer.

Raw sausage made from beef, pork, chicken or turkey, such as uncooked bratwurst, keeps 1 to 2 days in the refrigerator and 1 to 2 months in the freezer.

Deli meat or luncheon meat that has been opened or sliced at the deli counter keeps 3 to 5 days in the refrigerator. Unopened packaged luncheon meat keeps 2 weeks. Both keep 1 to 2 months in the freezer.

Hot dogs keep 1 week in the refrigerator once the package is opened and 2 weeks unopened. They keep 1 to 2 months in the freezer.

A fully cooked whole ham, store-wrapped, keeps 1 week in the refrigerator. A fully cooked half ham or ham slices keep 3 to 5 days. Both keep 1 to 2 months in the freezer.

A fresh, uncured, uncooked ham keeps 3 to 5 days in the refrigerator and about 6 months in the freezer.

Bacon keeps about 1 week in the refrigerator and 1 month in the freezer.
