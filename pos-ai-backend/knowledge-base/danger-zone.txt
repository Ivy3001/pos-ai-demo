Title: USDA Urges Consumers to Keep Clear of the Danger Zone
Source: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS)
URL: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/news-events/news-press-releases/usda-urges-consumers-keep-clear-danger-zone-summer
Checked: November 2026

The "Danger Zone" is the temperature range between 40°F and 140°F, where bacteria in food multiply fastest.

Perishable food such as raw meat, deli meat and cheese should not be left out in the Danger Zone for more than 2 hours. If the air temperature is above 90°F, for example at a summer cookout or in a hot car, the limit drops to 1 hour.

Cold food should be kept at 40°F or colder. Customers taking meat home on a long trip in hot weather can use a cooler with ice packs to keep it out of the Danger Zone.
